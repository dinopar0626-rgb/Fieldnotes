About this log

I document civil infrastructure across Tokyo as I encounter it — on site visits, during fieldwork, and in the drawings and standards behind what gets built. This log is a running record of that work: bridges, tunnels, seismic retrofits, transit infrastructure, and the reclaimed land the city stands on.

Each entry follows the same discipline as a site record: what the structure is, where it sits, what condition it's in, and what the fieldwork actually showed. Photographs are from site visits unless captioned otherwise. Specifications are drawn from public design documents and site signage, and are noted as such — always check primary sources before citing anything here professionally.

Get in touch if you spot an error, want to compare notes on a site, or have access to a location worth surveying.
